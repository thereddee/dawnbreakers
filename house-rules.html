House rules

Agreed to by everyone at the table, whether they remember agreeing or not.

  1. Phones go in the basket when we roll initiative. They come out when the session ends.
  2. The music never stops. No skipping, no shuffle, no crossfade. A track is allowed to finish.
  3. Everyone posts in the group chat when they get home. I am not losing a player to a snowbank. Or a ditch, Karine.
  4. Your dice are your own, until the DM has reason to think otherwise.
  5. Miss two sessions without warning and the gods will notice.
  6. Rules questions are welcome. The same rules question three weeks running is a lifestyle.
  7. The DM may change any rule if the story needs it. The DM decides what the story needs.
  8. What happens at the table stays at the table. Mostly.
Pizza is ordered for after the first scene. There is always a vegetarian one.

Session recaps

Book III: The Dawn Betrayed. What happened, as the DM remembers it. The DM remembers it correctly.

Year 611

Session 1 — 2 Emberfall. A half-orc named Bruga walks up the temple hill and into the story. High Priest Thorne blesses the party. Quartermaster Hobb finds boots in his size, which tells you most of what there is to know about Hobb.

Session 2 — 5 Emberfall. The Cult of the Hollow Sun shows its face in the Lower Town. The barbarian forgets to rage. We do not speak of it.

Session 3 — 9 Emberfall. The Feast of Embers. Thorne speaks, Hobb pours, the party sleeps at the temple. Ser Aldric keeps the Champion's vigil alone in the vault chapel until dawn.

Session 4 — 12 Emberfall. The north road. Wolves, and worse manners.

Session 5 — 20 Emberfall. An axe named Skullsong chooses its bearer.

Session 6 — 27 Emberfall. Vex of the Ninth Tower learns that the gods notice absences. A curse settles on her for a month.

Session 7 — 29 Emberfall. Into the barrow. The rogue's dice run suspiciously warm.

Session 8 — 3 Frostwane. Out of the barrow, minus some dignity.

Session 9 — 6 Frostwane. High Priest Thorne departs on pilgrimage to the Sunken Shrine. He will be back next month. Hobb keeps the temple in his absence.

Session 10 — 14 Frostwane. A night of resupply at the temple. Hobb hands out rations. Ser Aldric keeps vigil alone, a second time.

Session 11 — 21 Frostwane. Snow. The High Priest is still on the road. The river takes Skullsong. Rivers do that.

Year 612

Session 12 — 2 Thawrise. The new year opens in blood. Hobb Tallow dies in an ambush on the north road, and his key turns to ash in his hand. Thorne returns to bury him.

Session 13 — 8 Thawrise. The hunt for Hobb's killers begins.

Session 14 — 12 Thawrise. The cult's camp is found. Vex's curse lifts.

Session 15 — 17 Thawrise. The battle at the camp. The barbarian remembers to rage. There is cheering.

Session 16 — 22 Thawrise. New-year rites at the temple, Thorne presiding. Ser Aldric keeps vigil alone, a third time. The Champion is nothing if not devout.

Session 17 — 26 Thawrise. The road south.

Session 18 — 30 Thawrise. The Sunken Shrine and its bells. The wizard solves it. The wizard mentions this several times.

Session 19 — 3 Greenwake. The rogue rolls three natural twenties. The DM takes note.

Session 20 — 6 Greenwake. Rumours in the Lower Town: the temple is short of coin.

Session 21 — 8 Greenwake. The road home. Four natural twenties. The DM takes more notes.

Session 22 — 11 Greenwake. The quarterly count is read from the steps. The Vault is short by four thousand four hundred pieces of gold: three openings, by night, under no seal. Thorne hands the Champion a letter.

Session 23 — 13 Greenwake. Recap coming Sunday.

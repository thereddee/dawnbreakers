The Party

Four heroes. For now.

🛡

Ser Aldric Vane

Human paladin. Champion of the Dawn. Raised by the Order, sworn to honesty, courage and duty. Bearer of the third key.

Played by Maude, since Book I.

📖

Vex of the Ninth Tower

High elf wizard. Last scholar of a burned library. Corrects everyone, including the gods.

Played by Karine, since Book I.

🎲

Finch

Halfling rogue. Believes luck is a skill. The dice have so far agreed with him to a remarkable degree.

Played by Sam, since Book I.

🪓

Bruga Stonejaw

Half-orc barbarian. Likes the gods, his axe, and boots that fit. Currently between magic axes.

Played by Étienne, since Book III.

The Valelands

Eight valleys, one river, and more gods than anyone asked for.

The Valelands are a chain of river valleys between the Greyback mountains and the sea. No king has held all eight valleys for longer than a generation. What holds them together is older: the roads, the river, and the Temple of Dawn, whose Vault has kept the valleys' gold through every war.

Powers

The Order of the Dawn keeps the temple and the Vault. The Cult of the Hollow Sun teaches that the dawn is a lie told by the rich; it has been raiding the north road since last autumn. The valley lords stay out of both their ways.
